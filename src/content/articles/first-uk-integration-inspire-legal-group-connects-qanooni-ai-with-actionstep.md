# First UK Integration: Inspire Legal Group Connects Qanooni AI with Actionstep

Inspire Legal Group has become the first UK law firm to integrate Qanooni AI with Actionstep, according to Legal Futures. The integration embeds AI capabilities directly within the firm's practice management system rather than requiring lawyers to switch between separate applications.

The move represents a practical step beyond the pilot project phase that has characterised much legal AI adoption. Rather than testing tools in isolation, the firm is building AI into its existing operational infrastructure.

Qanooni AI provides legal research and document analysis capabilities, while Actionstep handles practice management functions including matter tracking, billing, and client communication. The integration allows lawyers to access AI tools without leaving their case management workflow.

I could not independently verify the technical specifications of the integration or the timeline for its implementation from the available source material. The announcement does not include details about which specific AI functions are available through the integration or how the data flows between the two systems.

Inspire Legal Group operates as a commercial law practice. The firm's decision to pursue this integration suggests confidence in both the reliability of the AI tools and the stability of the technical connection between the platforms.

The integration model may become more common as legal AI tools mature beyond standalone applications. Practice management systems already serve as central hubs for law firm operations, making them logical integration points for specialised AI capabilities.

*This integration approach could signal a shift from AI as an add-on tool to AI as embedded infrastructure, though the real test will be whether it actually improves day-to-day legal work.*
