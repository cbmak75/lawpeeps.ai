ClearyX, the alternative legal services provider spun out of top-tier New York firm Cleary Gottlieb, has launched CX+, a contract insights and due diligence platform that marks the ALSP's entry into the increasingly crowded legal AI market.

The platform joins a growing field of contract analysis tools as competition intensifies between traditional legal service providers, pure-play AI companies, and now the alternative legal services arms of major law firms.

According to Artificial Lawyer, which reported the launch, CX+ focuses on contract insights and due diligence work. The platform represents ClearyX's first major technology offering since the ALSP was established by its parent firm.

ClearyX's move into AI tooling reflects broader shifts in how law firms are positioning themselves in relation to legal technology. Rather than simply adopting third-party AI platforms, some firms are now developing or acquiring their own tools through their ALSP divisions.

The launch comes as contract AI has become one of the most competitive segments in legal technology, with established players like Kira Systems and newer entrants like Robin AI competing for market share alongside in-house tools developed by law firms and their service divisions.

For ClearyX, the platform represents an opportunity to leverage its parent firm's expertise in complex commercial transactions while offering AI-powered efficiency to a broader client base than Cleary Gottlieb's traditional roster.

The details of CX+'s capabilities and pricing were not disclosed in the initial announcement. I was unable to find additional technical specifications or client information on ClearyX's website at the time of writing.
