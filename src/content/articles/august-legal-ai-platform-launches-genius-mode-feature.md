# August Legal AI Platform Launches Genius Mode Feature

Legal AI platform August has launched Genius Mode, a new capability designed to enhance the platform's existing services for law firms.

The feature was demonstrated in a product walkthrough covered by Artificial Lawyer this week. According to the coverage, Genius Mode builds on August's existing platform to provide what the company describes as enhanced AI capabilities for legal work.

August joins a competitive field of legal AI platforms that have been rapidly adding features throughout 2025 and early 2026. The company has positioned itself as serving law firms specifically, though the exact scope of Genius Mode's functionality remains unclear from the available demonstration.

The timing coincides with increased activity across the legal AI sector, as platforms compete to differentiate their offerings beyond basic document processing and research functions that have become standard.

August has not yet published detailed specifications for Genius Mode or announced pricing changes related to the new capability. The company's existing platform serves law firms with AI-powered tools for various legal tasks.

Further technical details about Genius Mode's specific capabilities and implementation were not available in the demonstration coverage reviewed.
