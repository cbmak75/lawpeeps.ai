# Legora Signs Jude Law for 'Law Just Got More Attractive' Campaign

Legal AI provider Legora has signed actor Jude Law for a global brand campaign with the tagline "Law just got more attractive", according to an announcement made yesterday.

The celebrity endorsement deal represents a notable shift for the legal technology sector, where marketing budgets have traditionally focused on trade publications and conference sponsorships rather than mainstream brand building.

Legora, which provides generative AI tools for legal research and document drafting, has not disclosed the financial terms of the arrangement or the campaign's scope. The company did not immediately respond to requests for additional details about the campaign's launch timeline or media placement strategy.

The move comes as legal AI companies face intensifying competition for market share and client attention. Celebrity endorsements remain relatively uncommon in enterprise software marketing, though the practice has gained traction in consumer-facing fintech and productivity applications.

Legal IT Insider reported the announcement as part of what it described as growing brand investment across the sector, though specific comparisons with competitor spending were not provided in the available reporting.

The campaign's success will likely be measured not just in brand awareness metrics but in whether celebrity association can drive adoption among law firms that have remained cautious about AI implementation.
