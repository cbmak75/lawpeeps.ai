# Orbital Launches Real Estate Law Firm as Legal Tech Consolidation Continues

Orbital, the property technology company that focuses on residential sales, is launching its own law firm in the UK. The new firm will be called Farringdon, according to reports.

The move represents another step in the ongoing consolidation within legal technology, where companies are increasingly choosing vertical integration over partnerships with existing law firms.

Orbital operates in the property transaction space, providing technology to streamline residential sales processes. The decision to establish Farringdon suggests the company sees direct legal service provision as preferable to relying on referral relationships with independent conveyancing practices.

This follows a broader pattern in the legal tech sector where technology providers are moving beyond software-as-a-service models towards becoming direct service providers themselves. The approach allows companies to control the entire customer experience while potentially capturing more value from each transaction.

The property conveyancing market has been particularly active for legal tech investment in recent years, with several companies building tools to address the notoriously slow and opaque process of residential property transactions in England and Wales.

For Orbital, establishing its own law firm may provide more predictable service delivery and closer integration between its technology platform and the legal work itself. However, it also means taking on regulatory compliance obligations and the operational complexity of running a law practice.

The launch of Farringdon adds another data point to questions about whether legal tech companies ultimately view law firms as partners or as intermediaries to be replaced.

*The regulatory pathway for tech companies establishing law firms remains relatively straightforward in England and Wales, though the commercial wisdom varies considerably by sector. — mm!ke*
