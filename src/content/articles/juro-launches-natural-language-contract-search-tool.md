# Juro Launches Natural Language Contract Search Tool

Juro has launched Operator, a natural language interface that allows users to search and interrogate contract databases through conversational queries.

The tool provides what the company describes as "conversational access to a business's contracts and data" to help surface key information that might otherwise require manual review or complex database queries.

Juro, which focuses primarily on in-house legal teams, has positioned Operator as a way to make contract intelligence more accessible to non-lawyers within organisations. The feature sits within the company's existing contract lifecycle management platform.

The launch comes as natural language search capabilities become increasingly common across legal technology platforms, driven by advances in large language models and growing demand from legal teams for more intuitive ways to access their data.

Details about Operator's specific capabilities, pricing, or availability were not immediately available from the initial announcement.

*Juro's timing feels right. Natural language search has moved from experimental to expected, and contract data remains stubbornly difficult to access for most legal teams. mm!ke*
