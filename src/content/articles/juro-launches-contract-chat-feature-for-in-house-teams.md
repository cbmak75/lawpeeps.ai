# Juro Launches Operator Contract Chat for In-House Teams

Contract management platform Juro has launched Operator, a conversational interface that allows in-house legal teams to query their contract data using natural language.

The feature provides what Juro describes as "conversational access to a business's contracts and data" to help surface key information from contract repositories. According to initial reporting, the tool is designed specifically for in-house teams rather than law firms.

The launch represents Juro's latest move to integrate AI capabilities into its contract lifecycle management platform. The company has positioned itself as serving the in-house legal market, competing with providers like Ironclad and ContractWorks in the enterprise contract management space.

Details about Operator's technical implementation, pricing, or availability timeline were not immediately available. The conversational approach reflects a broader trend across legal technology, with providers adding chat-style interfaces to make contract and legal data more accessible to non-lawyer business users.

Juro, founded in 2016, has raised significant venture funding and serves enterprise customers across Europe and North America. The company's platform combines contract authoring, negotiation, and management tools with workflow automation.
